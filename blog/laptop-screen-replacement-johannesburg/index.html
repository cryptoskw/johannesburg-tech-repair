Laptop Screen Replacement in Johannesburg: What to Check First

What clients should know before booking a laptop screen replacement in Johannesburg.

A cracked screen is not always the only fault

When a laptop screen is visibly cracked, replacement is usually needed. But when there is no display, flickering, lines, dim light or a white screen, the technician must also check the screen cable, backlight, RAM, BIOS and external-display output.

Model number matters

Laptop screens are not universal. Size, connector type, resolution, refresh rate, brackets and touch capability must match. Send the exact model number before accepting a quote.

Business laptops and downtime

For business clients, the main cost is often downtime. If the laptop is used for work, mention urgency and whether an external monitor can be used temporarily.

Photos help the quote

Send a photo of the front screen, back cover, model sticker and any visible hinge damage. This makes it easier to quote the correct part and avoid delays.
