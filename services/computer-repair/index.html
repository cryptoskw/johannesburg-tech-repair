Desktop computer diagnostics

Desktop computers are often easier to repair than laptops, but the diagnosis still matters. A dead computer can be caused by a power supply, motherboard, RAM, storage drive, front-panel switch or shorted component. A slow office computer may need an SSD upgrade rather than a full replacement.

Business computer support

Businesses often need fast, practical help because a single faulty desktop can block invoices, emails, payroll or customer service. We focus on clear triage: what is urgent, what can be repaired, what should be upgraded and what should be replaced.

Typical services

We help with power faults, Windows boot problems, motherboard checks, RAM and SSD upgrades, data transfer, virus cleanup, fan replacement, software corruption and general performance optimization.

Good information saves time

When you enquire, include the desktop brand or custom build details, symptoms, any beeps or lights, whether the device was affected by load shedding, and whether important files are stored on the drive.
