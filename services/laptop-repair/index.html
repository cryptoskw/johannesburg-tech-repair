What we help with

Laptop faults can look simple from the outside but come from many different causes. A laptop that does not turn on may have charger, battery, DC jack, motherboard or liquid-damage issues. A laptop with no display may have a cracked panel, damaged screen cable, faulty RAM, BIOS corruption or board-level graphics faults. Johannesburg Tech Repair is designed to collect the right information before the device is opened so the repair path is clear.

Common laptop repair requests

Common enquiries include cracked laptop screens, loose hinges, slow Windows laptops, noisy fans, overheating gaming laptops, keyboard failures, battery replacement, charging problems, SSD upgrades, RAM upgrades, Windows reinstall and malware-related performance problems.

Before you book

Send the laptop brand, model number, photos of visible damage and a short description of what happened. If the data is important, say that clearly before any repair starts. We do not recommend wiping or reinstalling Windows before checking whether data backup is needed.

Repair process

The normal process is enquiry, device details, assessment, quote, approval, repair and collection/delivery arrangement. Where special parts are required, a deposit may be requested before ordering.
